# Follow-Up Questions

## 1. How large can the dataset be?

For this assessment, you can assume the dataset will remain relatively small (similar to the example provided). There are no strict performance requirements, so the focus should be on clarity, correctness, and structure rather than scalability.

That said, feel free to briefly mention how your solution could scale if needed.

## 2. Should the validation and business rules be configurable, or will they be fixed?

For this assignment, you can assume the rules are fixed.

However, we are interested in how you structure your solution, so if you choose to make the rules configurable or easily extendable, feel free to do so and explain your reasoning.

## 3. What is the acceptable format for the output?

The output should be a clear and structured list of detected clashes. JSON is preferred, as the API is intended to be consumed by other systems.

Each clash should include enough information for a consumer to understand the issue (e.g. involved buildings, type of violation, and a short description).

You are free to define the exact structure, as long as it is consistent and easy to interpret.
